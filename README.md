# Pengo Stats — Fast Online & Offline Status Checker

> **A simple, lightning-fast online and offline status checker for Discord bots and websites.**

Pengo Stats is built for one purpose: keeping track of whether your websites and bots are **ONLINE** or **OFFLINE** with real-time latency ping checks, clean OLED black styling, and zero bloated configurations.

---

## Brand Palette

- **Brand Purple**: `#9929EA` (accents, buttons, focus)
- **Online Green**: `#08CB00` (online indicators, passing checks)
- **Offline Red**: `#EF4444` (offline indicators, connection drops)
- **Pure Canvas**: `#000000` (OLED black)
- **Cards**: `#0D0D0D`
- **Borders**: `#242424`

---

## Features

- **Pure Online / Offline Detection**: Instant live checks for HTTP response status and reachability.
- **Websites & Discord Bots**: Add public endpoints, bot gateway ping URLs, or health links.
- **Real-Time Ping (Latency)**: Accurate response time in milliseconds.
- **Live "Check All" & Individual Check**: Trigger instant checks across all monitors or individually per service.
- **Search & Quick Filtering**: Filter by Online, Offline, Websites, or Discord Bots.
- **Local Storage**: Your monitors are persisted locally in browser storage without requiring any account or database.
